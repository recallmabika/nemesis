PONDAE — Accommodation Management

Off-Campus Student Housing Platform
Role Solo Full-Stack Build
Timeline 6+ months, ongoing
Category PropTech
Status Deployed on Azure

The Problem

Students looking for off-campus housing near Midlands State University had no reliable way to find verified rooms — listings were scattered across WhatsApp groups. Landlords tracked tenants, payments, and occupancy manually with no visibility into their own portfolio.

What We Built

  • Interactive property map: Geolocated housing listings with live filtering and distance-to-campus markers
  • Real-time chat: Instant tenant-to-admin messaging via Laravel Reverb / WebSockets
  • Analytics dashboard: Real-time tracking of revenue, occupancy rates, user growth, and conversion
  • Full tenant lifecycle: Room listing, allocation, automated lease onboarding, and payment verification

Case Study 05 Live & In Production

PONDAE Watcher — Network Activity Monitor

Tenant Network Visualization for Student Housing
Role Solo Full-Stack Build
Timeline Live, in active use
Category Network Monitoring
Status Deployed & Operational

The Problem

PONDAE's accommodation team needed a way to see and manage network activity across every tenant and student on the property — who was connected, how bandwidth was being used, and when something looked off — without walking floor to floor to check physical routers.

What We Built

  • Tenancy-wide network visualization: Every student's connected device activity rendered in a single unified view
  • Per-device Wi-Fi monitoring: Granular usage tracked down to MAC address and assigned room
  • Bandwidth limits & alerts: Automated flagging and throttling of unusual or excessive network usage
  • Intuitive owner dashboard: Purpose-built for property operations staff, avoiding complex networking jargon
